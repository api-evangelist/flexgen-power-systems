# FlexGen Power Systems

> FlexGen Power Systems is a Durham, North Carolina energy storage technology company founded in 2009.
> It designs, integrates and operates grid-scale battery energy storage systems (BESS) and the software
> that runs them — principally HybridOS, a hardware-agnostic energy management system (EMS) for
> multi-source, multi-site battery and solar portfolios.

## API status

FlexGen states on its own product pages that HybridOS offers "automated APIs" and, as of the 2026
release, "historian data API access". **No public developer portal, API reference, or machine-readable
specification is published.** As of 2026-08-16:

- No OpenAPI, Swagger, AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL, MCP server, or A2A agent card was found.
- `api.flexgen.com`, `docs.flexgen.com`, `developer.flexgen.com` and `developers.flexgen.com` do not
  resolve in DNS.
- `/openapi.json`, `/openapi.yaml`, `/swagger.json`, `/api-docs` and `/llms.txt` all return 404 on
  `www.flexgen.com`.
- Every `/.well-known/` path returns 404 on `www.flexgen.com`; the HTTP 200s on `portal.flexgen.com`
  and `trust.flexgen.com` are single-page-app catch-all HTML shells, not documents.

The HybridOS API surface is reached through customer deployments and the login-gated customer portal
at `portal.flexgen.com`. Treat FlexGen as having a **customer-only API**: real, but not publicly
documented.

## Products

- [HybridOS Energy Management System](https://www.flexgen.com/software/hybridos-energy-management-system): Hardware-agnostic EMS for multi-source, multi-site battery and solar energy management; instantaneous monitoring, web-based controls, automated APIs, historian data API access, integrated market prices, fleet-view mobile app.
- [HybridOS Control BMS](https://www.flexgen.com/software/hybridos-control-bms): US-built battery management system supporting numerous battery chemistries and manufacturer configurations, with pre-validated configurations for streamlined commissioning.
- [Solar Power Plant Controller (PPC)](https://www.flexgen.com/solar-power-plant-controller-ppc): Plant controller supporting Modbus, DNP3, CAN and synchrophasor protocols.
- [EMS Retrofit](https://www.flexgen.com/solutions/ems-retrofit): Replacement of an incumbent EMS on an existing storage site.
- [Data Center Solutions](https://www.flexgen.com/solutions/data-center-solutions): Battery storage for data-center interconnection and power.

## Integration surface

FlexGen's published integration surface is **industrial protocols, not web APIs**: Modbus, DNP3, CAN
and synchrophasor are named on the power plant controller page. FlexGen's GitHub organization
(`github.com/flexgen-power`) maintains a fork of the Go OPC UA library, indicating OPC UA is also in
the stack. No REST/GraphQL/event contract is published.
